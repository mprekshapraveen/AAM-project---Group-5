Democratizing Clean Solar Energy for Everyone

Bridging the gap for renters, flat owners, and shaded-roof residents through fractional community solar investment.


The Environmental Problem

Low Adoption of Renewable Energy[cite: 1]: Despite falling equipment costs and supportive schemes like PM Surya Ghar and ANERT Soura, voluntary rooftop solar adoption remains severely bottlenecked.

The primary driver is Structural Exclusion:

  • Urban renters and tenants lack property alteration rights.
  • Apartment dwellers lack individual terrace ownership.
  • Shaded or structurally weak roof owners cannot host systems independently.

This forces a major segment of the urban population to rely entirely on fossil-fuel-dominated grid power.

Target Users & Alignment

Target User Demographics:

  • Apartment & Flat Residents (e.g., Residents Welfare Associations).
  • Tenants and Urban Renters seeking green energy offsets.
  • Institutional clusters & households with unsuited roof structures.

Sustainability Alignment (SDG 7, 11, 13):

By enabling group investments, SolarShare directly aligns with SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy)[cite: 1] and SDG 13 (Climate Action) by replacing coal-heavy grid energy with localized, clean generation. It fulfills ISO 14001 continual improvement guidelines for residential clusters.

The Selected Engineering/IT Solution

SolarShare is a web platform leveraging Virtual Net-Metering (VNM) and fractional ownership ledgers to unite co-investors with available commercial/residential rooftop spaces.

1. Site Discovery & Solar API

Connects community roofs with open solar irradiance databases (NASA POWER API) to assess generation capacity and ideal kW installation sizes.

2. Fractional Ownership Ledger

Enables micro-investments down to fractions of a panel. Keeps a transparent, auditable breakdown of participant shares and ownership rights.

3. Virtual Net-Metering Allocation Engine

Calculates proportional grid-export credits and maps generation directly to DISCOM electricity bills or internal credit adjustments.

4. Financial & Subsidy Matcher

Automates payback calculations and integrates group subsidies under schemes like PM Surya Ghar and PM-KUSUM.

5. Installer & Vendor Portal

Allows MNRE-empanelled solar vendors to tender bids, ensuring quality installations and transparent warranties.

6. Real-Time CO₂ Dashboard

Provides co-investors live analytics on total kWh generated, grid cost savings, and collective metric tons of CO₂ avoided.
